# sana.run QA Report (QA-2609-11)

Manual testing defect report for spot and perps trading on sana.run, prepared by nayrbryanGaming.

- `index.html`: the report in English (default),
- `id.html` (served at `/id`): full Indonesian edition of the same report,
- `walkthrough.mp4`: 95 second muted walkthrough of the report with English and Indonesian captions,
- `tools/record-walkthrough.mjs`: script that records the walkthrough from the live page,
- the report page itself: with embedded highlight clips per defect and the complete muted recordings.
- `docs/QA-2609-11-sana-run-defect-report.docx`: Word edition of the same report.
- `clips/`: short muted clips cut to the key moments of each defect.
- `ev/`: evidence frames and screenshots.

13 defects (3 P1, 8 P2, 2 P3) and 11 improvement proposals. Every figure carries what it shows, severity, cause, possible impact, solution, evidence, steps to reproduce and feedback.

Independent QA report. Not an official Sana publication.
